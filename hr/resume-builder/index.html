    Frequently Asked Questions

    Common questions about resume builder

    How long should my resume be?
    ▾
    1 page for up to 5 years experience; 2 pages for 5+ years. Quality over length always.
    What makes a resume ATS-friendly?
    ▾
    Standard headings, no tables or images, clean fonts, and relevant keywords from the job description.
    Should I tailor my resume for each job?
    ▾
    Yes — especially the summary and skills section. Mirror keywords from the job description.
