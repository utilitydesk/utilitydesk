Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about experience letter generator

What is an experience letter?
▾
A certificate confirming that an employee worked at a company in a specific role for a stated period.
When should it be issued?
▾
On or after the last working day, once all exit formalities are complete.
Difference from a relieving letter?
▾
Relieving letter confirms departure and clearance. Experience letter certifies work history and duration.
