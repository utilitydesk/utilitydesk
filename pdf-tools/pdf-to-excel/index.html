Frequently Asked Questions

Does this upload my PDF?
No. The extraction workflow runs in your browser. The PDF is not sent to UtilityDesk.
Why can a complex table need cleanup?
PDFs store positioned text rather than spreadsheet cells. The tool infers rows and columns from text positions, so merged cells and unusual layouts may require manual Excel cleanup.
