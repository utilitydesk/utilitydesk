Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about ats checker

What is ATS?
▾
Applicant Tracking System — software used by 98% of large companies to filter resumes before a human sees them.
What kills ATS scores?
▾
Tables, columns, images, graphics, headers/footers with content, uncommon section titles, and complex formatting.
What ATS score is good?
▾
Above 75% is good. Above 90% is excellent. Below 60% means your resume may never reach a human recruiter.
