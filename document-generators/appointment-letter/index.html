Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about appointment letter generator

What is an appointment letter?
An appointment letter formally confirms employment after the candidate accepts the offer, with comprehensive terms including salary, probation, notice period, and company policies.
When is an appointment letter issued?
It is issued on or before the date of joining, after the candidate accepts the offer letter.
Is an appointment letter different from an offer letter?
Yes. An offer letter is issued before joining to extend the offer. An appointment letter is the formal employment contract issued when the employee joins.
