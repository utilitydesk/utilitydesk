Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about invoice generator

Is this invoice GST-compliant?
This generates a standard tax invoice format. Ensure you include your GSTIN, HSN/SAC code, and both CGST+SGST or IGST as applicable for full GST compliance.
What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?
CGST and SGST apply to intra-state (within same state) transactions, each at half the GST rate. IGST applies to inter-state transactions at the full GST rate.
Do I need to include HSN/SAC codes?
Yes, for full GST compliance. Add HSN (goods) or SAC (services) codes in the description field. HSN/SAC codes are mandatory for B2B invoices above ₹50,000.
