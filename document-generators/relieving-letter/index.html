RELIEVING LETTER

Date: —
To Whomsoever It May Concern

This is to certify that [Employee Name], working as [Designation] at [Company Name], has been relieved from the services of the company with effect from [Last Working Day] owing to [reason].

The employee joined us on [Start Date] and served until [Last Working Day].

[Employee Name] has been relieved from all duties and responsibilities. We confirm that there are no dues pending against them as on the date of relieving.

We wish them all the best in their future endeavours.

For [Company Name]

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about relieving letter generator

What is a relieving letter?
A relieving letter is issued by an employer on an employee's last working day, confirming that they have been officially relieved from their duties and responsibilities.
Is a relieving letter mandatory?
While not legally mandated, most companies require it during background verification. It is standard practice in India for all full-time employees.
Is it different from an experience letter?
Yes. A relieving letter confirms departure. An experience letter certifies work history and duration. Companies typically issue both.
