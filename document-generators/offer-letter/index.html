Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about offer letter generator

What is an offer letter?
▾
A formal document extending a job offer, outlining role, salary, joining date, and key conditions.
Is an offer letter legally binding?
▾
Yes, once accepted by the candidate it creates binding obligations under the Indian Contract Act 1872.
How do I download as PDF?
▾
Click "Print / PDF" and choose "Save as PDF" in your browser print dialog.
Difference between offer and appointment letter?
▾
Offer letter = sent before joining. Appointment letter = formal contract issued on joining day.
