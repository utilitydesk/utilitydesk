Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about purchase order generator

What is a purchase order?
A PO is a commercial document issued by a buyer to a seller, specifying the goods/services, quantities, agreed prices, and delivery/payment terms.
Is a purchase order legally binding?
Once accepted by the vendor, a PO becomes a legally binding contract. Keep a signed copy for records.
How is a PO different from an invoice?
A PO is issued by the buyer before delivery to authorise the purchase. An invoice is issued by the seller after delivery requesting payment.
