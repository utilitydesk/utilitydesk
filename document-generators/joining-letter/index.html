Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about joining letter generator

What is a joining letter?
▾
Sent to a selected candidate confirming joining date, reporting time, and list of documents to carry on Day 1.
How is it different from offer/appointment letters?
▾
Offer = job offer. Joining letter = Day-1 logistics. Appointment letter = formal employment contract on joining.
What documents should employees carry on Day 1?
▾
PAN, Aadhaar, educational certificates, experience letters, salary slips, passport photos, bank details.
