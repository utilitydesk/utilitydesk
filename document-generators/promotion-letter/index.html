Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about promotion letter generator

When should a promotion letter be issued?
▾
On or before the effective date of promotion, ideally a few days in advance.
What should it include?
▾
Old designation, new designation, effective date, revised CTC, and reason for promotion.
Is a promotion letter different from an increment letter?
▾
Promotion = change in role/title. Increment = salary revision without a title change.
