Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about resignation letter generator

How much notice should I give?
As per your employment contract — typically 30, 60 or 90 days. Check your offer letter or appointment letter for the exact notice period.
Should I mention the reason for resignation?
You are not legally required to state a reason, but it is professional to include a brief, positive reason.
Can I negotiate a shorter notice period?
Yes. Most companies allow buyout of the remaining notice period — your manager or HR can waive it by mutual agreement.
