Frequently Asked Questions

What is registration charges?
Registration charges are fees paid to the government to register the property sale deed. This makes the transaction legally valid and transfers ownership. In India, registration fee is typically 1% of property value.
Who pays registration charges?
Registration charges are typically paid by the buyer. Both buyer and seller must be present at the sub-registrar office for registration. The buyer bears the cost as it is essential for establishing legal ownership.
Is registration mandatory?
Yes, registration is mandatory for properties valued above â‚¹100 as per the Registration Act, 1908. Unregistered sale deeds are not legally valid and do not transfer ownership. Registration must be done within 4 months of execution.
What is the difference between stamp duty and registration?
Stamp duty is a tax on the transaction (4-8% of value). Registration fee is a charge for recording the transaction (1% of value). Both are mandatory. Stamp duty validates the document; registration makes it legally enforceable.
What documents are needed for registration?
Sale deed (on stamp paper), identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN), passport photos, property documents (title deed, encumbrance certificate), NOC from builder/society, and payment proof of stamp duty and registration fees.
