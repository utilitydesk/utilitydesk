What is rebate under Section 87A?

Who is eligible for rebate?
Resident individuals with total income below â‚¹7 lakh (new regime) or â‚¹5 lakh (old regime) are eligible for rebate. NRIs, HUFs, and firms are not eligible. The rebate is available only if taxable income (after deductions) is within the limit.

Is cess calculated on tax after rebate?
Yes, health and education cess of 4% is calculated on the tax amount after claiming rebate under Section 87A. So if rebate reduces tax to zero, cess is also zero. This is why individuals with income up to â‚¹7 lakh pay zero tax in new regime.
Can I claim rebate if I have TDS?
Yes, rebate is available regardless of TDS deducted. If TDS exceeds your actual tax liability (after rebate), you get a refund while filing ITR. The rebate reduces your tax liability, and excess TDS is refunded.
