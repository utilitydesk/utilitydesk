What Is an Experience Calculator?

An Experience Calculator computes your total work experience based on your first job start date and current/last working day. It accounts for career gaps between jobs and provides experience in years, months, and days.

This calculator is useful for resume writing, job applications, and understanding your career timeline. It also shows working days (excluding weekends) which some companies use for experience calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is work experience calculated?
Experience is calculated from your first job start date to your last working day (or today if currently employed). Career gaps between jobs are subtracted. Most companies count in years and months.
Do internships count as experience?
Full-time internships (3+ months) count as experience for freshers. Part-time internships may or may not count depending on the company. Always mention internships in your resume with duration.
Should I include notice period in experience?
Yes, notice period counts as experience since you are employed and receiving salary. If you are serving notice, your experience continues until the relieving date.
How do career gaps affect experience?
Career gaps reduce your total experience. If you worked 3 years, took 6 months break, then worked 2 more years, your total experience is 5 years (not 5.5 years). Be transparent about gaps in interviews.
Is experience calculated in calendar days or working days?
Most companies use calendar days (including weekends) for experience calculation. However, some government jobs count only working days. For resume purposes, use years and months based on calendar days.
