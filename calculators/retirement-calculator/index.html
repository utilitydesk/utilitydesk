Frequently Asked Questions

How much retirement corpus do I need?
A common rule is 25x your annual expenses (4% withdrawal rule). If you spend â‚¹50,000/month (â‚¹6L/year) today, at 6% inflation after 30 years you will need â‚¹34.8L/year, requiring â‚¹8.7 crore corpus.
When should I start saving for retirement?
As early as possible. Starting at 25 vs 35 can double your retirement corpus due to compounding. Even small amounts invested early grow significantly. Aim to invest 20-30% of income from your first job.
What is the best investment for retirement?
A mix of equity (mutual funds, NPS Tier 1), debt (PPF, EPF), and some gold. Young professionals (20s-30s) should have 70-80% equity. As you approach retirement, shift to debt for stability. NPS offers tax benefits.
How does inflation affect retirement?
Inflation erodes purchasing power. At 6% inflation, â‚¹50,000 today will cost â‚¹2.87 lakh after 30 years. Your retirement corpus must account for inflation to maintain lifestyle. This is why equity investments (beating inflation) are crucial.
Can I retire early (FIRE)?
Yes, with aggressive saving (50-70% of income) and disciplined investing, you can achieve financial independence by 45-50. Use the FIRE calculator to plan early retirement. The key is starting early and maintaining high savings rate.
