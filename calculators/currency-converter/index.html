What Is a Currency Converter?

A Currency Converter converts amounts between different world currencies using exchange rates. It supports major currencies like INR, USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, AUD, CAD, SGD, AED, and SAR.

Currency conversion is essential for international travel, online shopping, remittances, and business transactions. This calculator provides quick conversions with approximate exchange rates for reference.
