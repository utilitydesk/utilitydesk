OT Settings
How this calculator works: For each weekday (Mon–Sat), hours beyond the standard shift are counted as Daily OT. If total weekday hours also exceed the weekly normal threshold, any additional hours are counted as Weekly OT (excess over weekly standard that wasn't already caught by daily OT). Sunday hours always attract rest-day premium pay regardless of whether daily or weekly OT limits were reached. OT wages are included in ESI gross wages but excluded from PF basic wages — confirm with your payroll team.
