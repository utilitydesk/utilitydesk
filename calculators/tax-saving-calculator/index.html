What Is a Tax Saving Calculator?

A Tax Saving Calculator computes the total tax saved by claiming various deductions under the old tax regime. It shows your tax liability with and without deductions, helping you understand the benefit of tax-saving investments.

This calculator helps you plan your tax-saving strategy by showing the impact of 80C, 80D, HRA, home loan interest, and other deductions. It is useful for deciding whether to opt for the old or new tax regime.

Tax Saving Formula

Taxable Income = Gross Income - Standard Deduction - All Deductions
Tax Payable = Tax on Taxable Income + Cess (4%)
Tax Saved = Tax Without Deductions - Tax With Deductions
Taxable Income = After all deductionsTax Payable = As per slabs + cessTax Saved = Benefit of deductions
