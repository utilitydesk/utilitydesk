What Is a Balance Transfer Calculator?

A Balance Transfer Calculator compares your existing loan with a new lender's offer to determine if switching saves money. It factors in the interest rate difference, processing fees, and remaining tenure to show your net savings.

Balance transfers are most beneficial when interest rates have fallen since you took the original loan, or when you find a significantly better offer. Even a 0.5% rate reduction can save lakhs over the remaining tenure.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I do a balance transfer?
When the new rate is at least 0.5-1% lower than your current rate, and the savings exceed the transfer costs (processing fee, foreclosure charges). Best done early in the loan tenure when interest component is high.
What are the costs of balance transfer?

Can I do a balance transfer for any loan?
Yes, for home loans, car loans, personal loans, and business loans. Floating-rate loans have zero foreclosure penalty. Fixed-rate loans may charge 2-4%. Check both lenders' terms before transferring.
