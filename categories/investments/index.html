Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about investment calculators

What is the best investment for long-term wealth?
Equity mutual funds through SIP have historically delivered 12-15% annualized returns over 10+ years. They provide diversification, professional management, and the power of compounding.
How much should I invest for retirement?
Aim to invest 15-20% of your income consistently. Use our retirement calculator to determine the exact corpus needed based on your expected lifestyle and inflation.
What is the difference between growth and dividend options?
Growth option reinvests all returns, benefiting from compounding. Dividend option pays out periodic income but doesn't compound. For long-term wealth, growth option is better.
