Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about business calculators

What is ROI and how do I calculate it?
ROI (Return on Investment) measures the profitability of an investment. Formula: ROI = (Net Profit / Cost of Investment) × 100. Our ROI calculator helps you quickly compute returns for business projects, marketing campaigns, and investments.
How do I calculate break-even point?
Break-even point is when total revenue equals total costs. Formula: Break-Even Units = Fixed Costs / (Selling Price - Variable Cost). Use our break-even calculator to find exactly how many units you need to sell to cover all costs.
What is the difference between markup and margin?
Markup is the percentage added to cost to get selling price. Margin is the percentage of profit relative to selling price. For example, if cost is ₹100 and selling price is ₹150: Markup = 50%, Margin = 33.3%.
How do I calculate GST on my products?
GST is calculated as a percentage of the base price. For intra-state sales, split into CGST and SGST (equal halves). For inter-state sales, charge IGST (full amount).
