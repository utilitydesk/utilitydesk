Frequently Asked Questions

Which tax regime is better for me?

The new tax regime is generally better if you don't have significant deductions under Section 80C, 80D, HRA, etc. The old regime is better if you claim multiple deductions totaling more than â‚¹2-3 lakhs. Use our Income Tax Calculator to compare both regimes with your actual income and deductions.

How much should I invest in NPS?

Financial advisors recommend investing 10-15% of your gross income in retirement funds like NPS. You can claim additional tax deduction of â‚¹50,000 under Section 80CCD(1B) beyond the â‚¹1.5 lakh limit of Section 80C. Our NPS Calculator can help you estimate returns based on your contribution amount.

What is the ideal EMI to income ratio?

Financial experts recommend keeping your total EMI obligations (home loan, car loan, personal loan, credit card) below 40-50% of your monthly income. This ensures you have enough for savings, emergencies, and other expenses. Use our EMI Calculator to plan your loan repayments wisely.

When can I withdraw from PF?

You can make partial withdrawals from PF for specific purposes like medical emergency, marriage, house construction, or after 1 month of unemployment. Full withdrawal is allowed at retirement (age 58) or after 2 months of unemployment. Check our PF Withdrawal Guide for detailed rules and conditions.
